Spooky Season Bingo

Enter your name, choose up to 3 cards, and play along as Halloween words, horror films, and fall favorites are called.

How points work

BINGO claims add points to your score. Points decide the final winners, so keep playing after every BINGO.

  • Rounds 1-3: any row, column, or diagonal is 100 points.
  • Round 3 is the final round: regular BINGO plus a 200-point X Bingo bonus.
  • Round 2 adds a 50-point Four Corners bonus. Each round lasts 20 minutes, and new cards are dealt when the next round begins. The loudest table wins a prize at the end, so yell BINGO and make noise every time.
